# SPOTRY Stage 8 Photography Direction v1

Decision date: 2026-09-22
User-selected direction: Option 1 — action-first sports editorial.

## Locked visual rule
- Prioritize the movement, body mechanics, equipment, texture, speed, and physical environment of the sport over faces.
- Faces may appear naturally but are not the focal point.
- Use strong crop, authentic motion, directional light, and real sport context.
- Avoid generic smiling lifestyle portraits, posed gym selfies, influencer framing, and stock-photo handshake energy.
- Home hero may use editorial/prototype imagery, but must be labeled internally as prototype imagery and never imply a real provider.
- Provider/facility/session/review proof surfaces require actual supply photography in production.
- Image treatment must remain compatible with Direction B: white canvas, dark typography, Action Green accent, minimal shadow.

## Surface application
- Home hero: full-bleed action-first image with dark readability scrim; copy remains dominant.
- Explore cards: action crop leads; metadata remains readable and availability truth is never placed over ambiguous imagery.
- Experience detail: sport action image can lead, followed by real venue/provider evidence blocks.
- Provider trust: documentary imagery only; editorial prototype assets prohibited as evidence.
- Record/Compare: imagery is secondary to user evidence and metrics.

## Accessibility / content rules
- Alt labels describe the sport action, not inferred identity or emotion.
- Do not infer skill, qualification, safety, or provider verification from a photo.
- Do not use generated/editorial images as documentary proof.

## QA
Reject an image if the first impression is primarily a model/face rather than the sport action, if the crop hides the sport context, or if the image could be mistaken for proof of a real provider.
